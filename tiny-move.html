Tiny Move Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Tiny Move (“the App”), developed and published by Jensen (“the Developer”). Tiny Move helps users manage gentle movement, eye-break, hydration, and activity reminders using settings and data processed on the user's device.

2. Data Stored Locally

Tiny Move stores reminder settings, daily rhythm preferences, pause status, reminder interaction history, language and appearance preferences, and limited app state on your device. This information is used only to provide reminder features.

The Developer does not operate servers that collect, store, analyze, or share Tiny Move records.

3. Notifications

Tiny Move may send optional reminder notifications based on your settings. Notification content and actions such as “Snooze” or “Skip today” are processed locally on your device and are not used for advertising, tracking, or developer-side analytics.

4. Activity Recognition and Reminders

When the user enables Move reminders and grants activity recognition permission, Tiny Move uses Android Activity Recognition to receive inferred activity states such as walking, moving, still, or in a vehicle.

These activity states are used only to:

  • Pause inappropriate sedentary reminders while the user is moving or travelling in a vehicle.
  • Restart the sedentary reminder timer after the user becomes still again.
  • Display local motion diagnostics and an estimated continuous stillness duration on the Status screen.

Tiny Move does not access Health Connect, step counts, location, or raw accelerometer data. Activity recognition data is not used for medical diagnosis, health scoring, advertising, or location tracking, and is not uploaded or shared with third-party servers.

5. Data Sharing, Analytics, and Advertising

The Developer does not sell Tiny Move data or share it with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or developer-operated external servers. Tiny Move contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK.

Tiny Move does not provide native direct integration with wearable manufacturers. If your device supports watch alerts, notifications are delivered through the phone's notification synchronization mechanism.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Reminder settings, daily rhythm settings, reminder interaction history, scheduling state, and activity states and timestamps derived from Activity Recognition are stored only on the user's device.

This local data is retained until the user clears Tiny Move's app data or uninstalls the app. Users may revoke the activity recognition permission at any time through Android system settings. After permission is revoked, Tiny Move stops receiving new activity states and uses fixed-interval reminders instead.

Tiny Move has no user accounts, in-app cloud synchronization, or server-side database. This data is not sold, uploaded, or shared with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Tiny Move is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
