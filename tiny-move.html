Tiny Move Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Tiny Move, a lightweight reminder and activity-support app published by Jensen. Tiny Move is designed to help users keep small movement, stretch, or activity habits with simple local controls.

2. Data You May Enter or Create

Tiny Move may allow users to create or adjust reminder settings, schedule preferences, activity labels, local progress status, or app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's reminder and activity-support features.

3. Local Storage

Tiny Move stores user settings and app state locally on the user's device or local app storage environment. Jensen does not operate a separate server to collect, store, analyze, or share Tiny Move records.

4. Notifications

Tiny Move may provide optional reminder notifications. Reminder notifications are generated from the user's settings and are not used for tracking, advertising, or developer-side analytics.

5. Data Sharing

Jensen does not sell Tiny Move data. Jensen does not share Tiny Move data with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or developer-owned external servers.

6. Analytics and Advertising

Tiny Move does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not use user activity, reminder settings, or app usage for advertising, profiling, or selling user data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Tiny Move does not collect personal data on a developer-owned server, there is no developer-side data retention. Users can remove local app data by clearing app storage, resetting app settings, disabling the app, or uninstalling the app.

8. Children's Privacy

Tiny Move is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.
