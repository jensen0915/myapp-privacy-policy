Privacy Policy for Luma Breath

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Luma Breath is developed by Jensen. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles information.

1. Data Collection

Luma Breath does not collect, upload, or transmit personal or sensitive user data.

The application does not require an account and does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Third-party sign-in
  • Location information
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts or photos
  • Health platforms or Health Connect data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its core functionality, Luma Breath stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Breathing practice history
  • Weekly practice totals and streaks
  • Session duration and breathing pace preferences
  • Sound, ambient sound, and haptic preferences
  • Character illustration preference
  • Language preference

This information is used only by the application on your device. The developer cannot view or access it.

3. System Backup

Depending on your Android device and Google Account backup settings, Android may include some application data in a system backup.

These backups are managed by Android or your device service provider. The developer does not directly receive or access their contents. You can manage or disable backup through your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

Luma Breath does not sell, rent, or share your data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

5. Permissions

The application uses vibration permission only to provide optional haptic breathing cues.

You can disable haptic cues in the application settings. This permission is not used to collect or transmit data.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored information by:

  • Clearing the application's storage or data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the application

Once deleted, locally stored practice history and preferences cannot be recovered. If Android system backup is enabled, management and deletion of backup data are subject to Google's or your device service provider's settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Luma Breath does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children.

If the application is designated as targeting children in the future, this policy will be updated to comply with applicable Google Play Families policies and relevant laws.

8. Health Disclaimer

Luma Breath provides general breathing exercises and relaxation support. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you experience dizziness, chest discomfort, breathing difficulty, or other discomfort during a session, stop immediately and seek medical assistance when appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

If future versions introduce features that affect data handling, such as cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party services, this privacy policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated.

Changes will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
